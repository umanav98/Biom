About BiomStudio

We are passionate architects dedicated to creating sustainable, innovative spaces that inspire and endure.

Our Story

Founded in 2015 by visionary architect Sarah Chen, BiomStudio emerged from a simple yet powerful belief: architecture should harmonize with nature, not compete against it. What started as a small design studio has grown into a leading firm specializing in sustainable architecture and innovative design solutions.

Our journey began with a single residential project that challenged conventional building practices. We discovered that by working with natural elements rather than against them, we could create spaces that were not only beautiful and functional but also environmentally responsible.

Today, BiomStudio has completed over 150 projects across residential, commercial, and institutional sectors, each one a testament to our commitment to sustainable design and client satisfaction.

Our Mission

To create innovative, sustainable architecture that enhances human experience while respecting and preserving our natural environment. We believe every building should contribute positively to its community and ecosystem.

Our Vision

To be the leading force in sustainable architecture, setting new standards for environmental responsibility and design excellence. We envision a future where every building is a testament to harmony between human needs and natural systems.

Meet Our Founder

The visionary behind BiomStudio's innovative approach

Sarah Chen, AIA, LEED AP

Founder & Principal Architect

Sarah Chen is a registered architect with over 15 years of experience in sustainable design and green building practices. She holds a Master of Architecture from MIT and is a LEED Accredited Professional with expertise in energy-efficient design.

Before founding BiomStudio, Sarah worked with renowned firms including Foster + Partners and Bjarke Ingels Group, where she contributed to award-winning projects that pushed the boundaries of sustainable architecture.

Sarah's design philosophy centers on the belief that great architecture emerges from understanding the unique relationship between site, climate, and human needs. Her work has been featured in Architectural Record, Dwell, and Green Building & Design.
