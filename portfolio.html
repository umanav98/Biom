Featured Project Details

In-depth look at some of our most innovative designs

EcoVilla Residences

Location: Portland, Oregon

Year: 2023

Size: 45,000 sq ft

Certification: LEED Platinum

This groundbreaking residential complex features 24 units designed around a central courtyard. Each unit includes solar panels, rainwater collection systems, and energy-efficient appliances. The building's orientation maximizes natural light while minimizing heat gain.

  • • 100% renewable energy
  • • 40% reduction in water usage
  • • Green roof with native plants
  • • Electric vehicle charging stations

TechHub Office Complex

Location: San Francisco, California

Year: 2022

Size: 120,000 sq ft

Certification: LEED Gold

Designed specifically for technology companies, this office complex features flexible workspaces, collaborative areas, and cutting-edge technology integration. The building's design promotes innovation and employee well-being.

  • • Smart building automation
  • • Flexible workspace configurations
  • • Rooftop garden and recreation area
  • • Advanced air filtration systems
