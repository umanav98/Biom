Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about our services and process

How long does a typical project take?

Project timelines vary based on complexity and size. A simple residential project may take 3-6 months, while larger commercial projects can take 12-18 months from concept to completion.

Do you work with specific contractors?

We can recommend trusted contractors we've worked with, but you're free to choose your own. We provide construction administration services regardless of your contractor choice.

What's included in sustainable design?

Our sustainable design services include energy modeling, renewable energy integration, water conservation systems, sustainable material selection, and LEED certification assistance.

Can you help with permits and approvals?

Yes, we provide comprehensive permit assistance including preparing required documentation, coordinating with local authorities, and ensuring compliance with building codes.
