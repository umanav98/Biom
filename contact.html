Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about our services

How much does an architectural consultation cost?

Our initial consultation is free for projects over $50,000. For smaller projects, we offer a $200 consultation fee that can be applied to your project if you choose to work with us.

How long does the design process take?

Design timelines vary by project complexity. A simple residential project typically takes 3-6 months, while larger commercial projects can take 12-18 months from concept to construction completion.

Do you work on projects outside your local area?

Yes, we work on projects nationwide. While we're based in Creative City, we regularly travel for projects and can coordinate remotely for certain phases of the design process.

What information should I bring to the initial consultation?

Bring your project ideas, site information (if available), budget range, timeline, and any inspiration images. The more information you provide, the better we can understand your vision.
